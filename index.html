About us

Who we are.

Arbuda Plastic is a Vasai-based manufacturer and bulk wholesaler of PVC and EVA footwear. Established in 2014, we specialize in durable, comfortable and stylish footwear including men's and ladies' slippers, EVA sandals, designer chappals and school shoes.

What we do

We manufacture PVC and EVA footwear with advanced production capabilities and a commitment to strict quality control. We are a trusted OEM partner and wholesale supplier for businesses across India.

Why businesses choose us

  • Transparent dealing and competitive bulk pricing

  • OEM-ready for private-label requirements
