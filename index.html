Who we are.

Arbuda Plastic has manufactured PVC and EVA footwear at our Vasai East factory since 2014. Over a decade supplying wholesale distributors, OEM brands, and bulk buyers across India.

From granule to dispatch.

Every pair is made end-to-end at our Sativali factory. Here is how an order moves through the floor.

What we make

PVC and EVA footwear — slippers, sandals, and school shoes — produced at our Sativali, Vasai facility with quality checks at every production stage. 40+ active styles for wholesale and OEM buyers.

Why businesses choose us

  • Transparent dealing and competitive bulk pricing
  • Fast, reliable order fulfilment and delivery
  • OEM-ready for private-label requirements
