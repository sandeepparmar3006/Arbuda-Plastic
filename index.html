About us

Who we are.

Arbuda Plastic is a Vasai-based manufacturer and bulk wholesaler of PVC and EVA footwear. Established in 2014, we specialise in durable, comfortable and stylish footwear — men's and ladies' slippers, EVA sandals, designer chappals and school shoes.

✓ 10+ Years Manufacturing
✓ OEM & Private Label
✓ Bulk Wholesale Pricing
✓ PAN India Delivery

What we make

Advanced PVC and EVA footwear manufactured in our Vasai East facility with strict quality control at every stage. Trusted by wholesale buyers and OEM brands across India.

Why businesses choose us

  • Over a decade of manufacturing experience since 2014
  • Transparent dealing and competitive bulk pricing

  • OEM-ready for private-label requirements
